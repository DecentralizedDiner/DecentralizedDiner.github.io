Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Email Magic Outreach is a personal tool with one user, its owner Vrajesh Shah (vrajesh.web3@gmail.com).

What data the app accesses

  • Gmail: sends emails from the owner's account and reads the owner's own inbox to detect replies and bounces.
  • Google Sheets: reads and writes the owner's private outreach spreadsheet.
  • Google Drive: reads the owner's resume files to attach them to emails.

How data is used

Data is used only to send and track the owner's own outreach emails. It is stored only in the owner's Google account and on the owner's computer.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
